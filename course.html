Introduction

This paragraph stands in for professionally authored content. It exists so the reading experience can be judged: measure, line height, heading rhythm, the size of touch targets, and how a reference reads on a phone between patients.

A biokineticist reads this one-handed, in a corridor, with a patient waiting. Nothing in the layout should require zooming, horizontal scrolling or a second hand.

Core content

Clinical authors define anatomical structures, explain their relevance to practice, and link each claim to peer-reviewed evidence. Illustrations carry alternative descriptions, never colour alone, and a labelled diagram is treated as content with its own rights check.
