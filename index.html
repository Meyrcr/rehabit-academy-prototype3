One line, five stages

A practitioner does not buy an article. They buy the record an article ends in. Each stage below is a screen in this prototype — except the last, which is deliberately empty.

01

Read the lesson

Learning text, references and labelled diagrams in a reader built for a phone at 66 characters per line. The accreditation status of the activity sits above the title, not in a footnote.

Open the reader chevron_right

02

Answer ten questions

Multiple choice, keyboard operable, one question on screen at a time on a phone. The attempt counter, the timer and the pass mark are shown before the first question, not after the last.

Try the practice test chevron_right

03

Pass, or use the second attempt

A proposed 80% pass mark and two attempts, with a server-enforced lock between them. This prototype marks the test in your browser to show the interaction; production marking is server-side and transactional.

The record is the product

A CPD year is a list with dates on it. Rehabit Academy keeps that list as evidence: who did what, on which version of the activity, under which approval, and what was submitted.

  • Completion history per practitioner, per activity version.
  • Accreditation snapshot stored with the award, so a later re-rating cannot rewrite history.
  • Evidence register export for the provider, separate from the learner's certificate.

Three categories open, one closed

The Academy will not sell a lesson as CPD before an accreditor has approved it. That rule is a switch in the product, so the catalogue is the honest place to look for it.
